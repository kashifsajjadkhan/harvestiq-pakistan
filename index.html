— Fractional Green Canopy Cover

Measure green canopy cover from field photos.

This toolkit provides lightweight applications for quantifying fractional green canopy cover from downward-facing images — built for researchers, extension agents, and educators who need a reliable, fast, and fully offline tool to analyze images in the office or in the field. In all these tools, images remain on your device.

📱 HarvestIQ-Field
Ideal for smartphone use. Add comments, compute metrics, and track crop/soil conditions.
📤 HarvestIQ-Drag&Drop
Batch analysis in your browser, extracts metadata, and creates overlay masks.
💡 HarvestIQ-Advisory
Integrated real-time agronomic recommendations and crop health assessments.

🔒 Private & On-Device

Analyze unlimited images of any size. No account, no cloud — your data stays private.

🎛️ Adjustable Thresholds

Customizable ratios, opacity, and mask colors to inspect classification instantly.

📊 Multiple Outputs

Tabular results, binary masks, Pseudo-NDVI, and blended overlays.

⚡ High-Speed Processing

Process at full resolution or optimized for fast field batches.

📍 Image Metadata

Filename, timestamp, GPS coordinates, and device tracking integrated.

🔍 Noise Filtering

Removes small isolated specks and soil artifacts from the classification.
